    How RoomSync works

    Everything you need to know, from your first room to your last deadline.

    What RoomSync is

    RoomSync is a shared deadline board for a class. You and your classmates join one room, add the deadlines you know about, and everyone sees the same trusted list: assignments, quizzes, exams and more, on a weekly board and a calendar.

    The twist is approval. A deadline someone adds only shows up for the whole room after a room admin approves it, so the board stays accurate instead of filling up with guesses.

    Getting started

    • Sign up with your name, email and a password. Forgot it later? Use “Forgot password” on the log in page and we email you a reset link.
    • Create a room for your class, or join one with the invite code a classmate shares. You can be in more than one room and switch between them from the room button.
    • Admin or member? Whoever creates a room is its admin. Admins approve deadlines. Members add deadlines, tick them off, chat and share notes.

    Adding deadlines

    Press New TODO, pick the room, give it a title and a due date and time, and choose a type: exam, quiz, case study, activity, seatwork or assignment. Need something else? Use a custom tag. You can add a note with details, and add several titles in one go.

    A new deadline waits as pending until an admin approves it.

    Approving

    The Approve page lists what is waiting. Admins can approve or reject each one, or use Approve all for the whole list. Approved deadlines appear for everyone, and the history keeps a record of what was decided.

    Home board

    Home shows the week at a glance, one column per day, with the approved deadlines for your room. Tick a task when you finish it, open it to see its details and notes, and use search to find a task fast.

    Calendar

    The calendar shows the same approved deadlines by month, colour coded by type, so you can see a busy week coming before it arrives.

    Discussion

    Each room has a chat for the class. It is for talking things over, like asking what a quiz covers. Room chat is not end-to-end encrypted, so do not share passwords or anything private there.

    Notes and files

    Upload PDFs, Word documents and code files for your room, up to 10 MB each. Every file is checked before it is stored and is only ever offered as a download, never run.

    Profile and settings

    • Status: tell the room what you are up to. The dot on your picture is green for Working, yellow for BRB, red for Somewhere Else and grey for a custom status.
    • Photo and room icon: add a profile picture, and admins can set an icon for the room.
    • Settings: change your password, manage your rooms, or leave a room.

    Quick answers

    Is it free?

    Yes. RoomSync is a student project from Holy Angel University.

    How many people fit in a room?

    Up to 50 members.

    Who can see my deadlines?

    Only members of the room. Nothing in a room is visible to people outside it.
