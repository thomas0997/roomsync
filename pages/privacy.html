RoomSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

RoomSync helps university classmates organize deadlines, share tasks, and communicate in rooms. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have. If you have questions, contact thomas@roomsync.me.

1. Who We Are

RoomSync is a web app operated by two student developers based in Angeles City, Philippines. It is not currently operated by a registered company.

Under the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the RoomSync operators act as the personal information controller for the personal data processed through the app. This means we are responsible for deciding why and how your personal data is used.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to operate RoomSync:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, password hash, optional status text, and account creation date. Passwords are stored as hashes, not in readable form.
  • Room information: The rooms you belong to and your role in each room, such as admin or member.
  • Task information: Task titles, types, subjects, due dates, who created and approved each task, and your personal completion or removal marks.
  • Discussion messages: The text you post, your account identity, and the time of your message.
  • Technical information: Your IP address, request time, and request path in basic server logs. We do not log discussion message content in these logs.
  • Login information: A login token stored in your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Let users join and manage rooms.
  • Submit, approve, display, and track tasks.
  • Display discussion messages.
  • Keep the service working and investigate technical problems or security issues.
  • Respond to privacy requests and other user concerns.

We do not use your personal data for advertising or behavioral analytics.

4. Who Can See Your Information?

Room members can see the approved tasks and discussion messages in their rooms. Room admins can manage tasks and room membership.

Room chat is not end-to-end encrypted. Other members and admins can read messages in their room, and the RoomSync operators can technically access stored messages. Do not post information you would not want other room members to see.

We use Render for server hosting in Singapore and Neon for our PostgreSQL database. These providers process or store information on our behalf to operate RoomSync. Depending on their infrastructure and backup arrangements, your information may be processed or stored outside the Philippines.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics trackers, or third-party advertising cookies.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We store passwords as hashes and limit access to our systems. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your login token is stored in your browser’s local storage. Anyone who obtains a valid token may be able to access your account. Log out when using a shared device and keep your device secure.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

When we establish our retention schedule, it will cover account records, server logs, backups, tasks, and discussion messages.

Historical tasks and messages may remain after an account is deleted to preserve room history. We will remove or anonymize personal information when it is no longer needed, where appropriate and technically possible.

Information retained in backups may take additional time to disappear as those backups expire.

7. Deleting Your Account and Leaving Rooms

You can delete your account through Settings. We will remove your login credentials, email address, optional status, and room memberships from our active account records.

If you are the last member of a room, the room and everything in it is deleted.

On historical tasks and messages that we retain, your name will be replaced with “Deleted user.” The task details and message text may remain visible to the room’s members. Other people may still recognize you from the content or its context, so this does not guarantee complete anonymity.

We may retain limited information when necessary to address security issues, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

You can leave a room at any time. Leaving removes your membership, but content you previously posted may remain visible to that room. Room admins can also remove members.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have rights concerning your personal information, subject to applicable legal conditions. These include the rights to:

  • Be informed about how your information is collected and used.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Object to certain forms of processing.
  • Request the blocking or deletion of information when legally justified.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable law.

To ask what information we hold, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email thomas@roomsync.me. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as RoomSync changes. We will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
